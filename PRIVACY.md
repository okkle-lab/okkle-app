# Okkle Privacy Notice

Effective date: 2026-06-29

Okkle is designed as an on-device mileage, income, expense, and tax estimate tool for self-employed UK couriers. Okkle does not run a backend service for your app data, does not sell data, and does not upload your courier records, receipts, location history, or tax details to Okkle Lab servers.

## Data Stored On Your Device

Okkle may store the following data locally on your phone:

- Profile details you enter, such as name, vehicle, platforms, tax region, other income estimate, UTR, National Insurance number, and address.
- Trip and mileage records, including timestamps, distance, route breadcrumbs, platform, vehicle, and optional earnings.
- Income and expense records, including receipt images you choose to attach.
- App settings, reminders, onboarding state, backup/restore state, and lightweight diagnostics.

This data stays on the device unless you choose to export, share, back up, restore, or email it.

## Permissions

Okkle asks for permissions only for app features you use:

- Location, including background location, to track delivery trip distance and continue an active trip while the phone is locked or the app is backgrounded.
- Motion and Fitness to help detect when you may have started driving so Okkle can show an optional trip nudge.
- Camera and photo library to attach receipt or pay-statement images and read receipt text on device.
- Calendar write access to add HMRC Self Assessment and Making Tax Digital deadline reminders.
- Notifications to send logging reminders, deadline reminders, and active trip status updates.
- Mail/share capabilities so you can send feedback, diagnostics, backups, CSV exports, PDFs, and Accountant Packs.

## Exports, Backups, And Sharing

You control every export and share action. Accountant Packs, CSV files, backup files, diagnostics, and feedback emails leave your phone only when you choose to send or save them through iOS sharing, Mail, Files, iCloud, AirDrop, or another destination you select.

Backup files may contain the data you have stored in Okkle. Keep them somewhere you trust.

## Feedback And Diagnostics

If you report a problem or suggest an improvement, Okkle can compose an email to `admin@okklelab.com`. Diagnostics are intentionally minimal and include app version, device model, OS version, screen name, timestamp, and recent local error messages. They do not include your financial records, receipt images, or location history unless you explicitly attach a screenshot yourself.

## Third-Party Services

Okkle uses Apple and Expo/React Native platform APIs to provide device features such as location, maps, camera, calendar, notifications, printing, file sharing, and email composition. Data shared through those system services is handled according to the destination you choose and that provider's policies.

Okkle does not currently use analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, crash-reporting SDKs, payment SDKs, or a remote database for your records.

## Deleting Data

You can delete your Okkle data from the app's Data & backup settings. You can also delete the app from your device to remove its local app container data, subject to normal iOS behavior for files you already exported or backed up elsewhere.

## Contact

For privacy questions, contact `admin@okklelab.com`.
